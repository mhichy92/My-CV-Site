Faculty Annual Report

My CV (Symplectic Elements) is BU’s faculty research profiling system. The Faculty Annual Report (FAR) and External & International Activity Report (EIAR) you complete each January are generated directly from your My CV profile. Faculty who maintain their profile throughout the year complete both reports in under an hour. The most common source of reporting-season stress is a profile that has not been updated since the previous year.

How the reporting cycle works
Year-Round 📁

Maintain Your Profile

Add scholarly works, service roles, grants, and professional activities as they happen throughout the year.

January 📋

Reports Open

The FAR and EIAR become available. Your pre-populated profile is the starting point, not a blank form.

February ✅

Submit by Unit Deadline

Most school deadlines fall in February. Your unit sets its own deadline. Check with your department.

Before you click Start

Your Pre-Flight Checklist

Steps 1 and 2 are year-round maintenance tasks. Step 3 is the January submission. The more current your profile, the faster your submission.

Good to know before you start

How My CV decides what appears in your FAR

My CV uses the dates you enter on each activity to determine whether it appears in your FAR. If an activity has no end date, it will carry forward into every FAR until you add one — this is intentional for ongoing roles like committee memberships, but can cause confusion if you have completed activities with missing end dates. Before starting your FAR, scan your profile and add end dates to any activities that are no longer ongoing.

1
Year-Round
Scholarly & Creative Works

Claim or reject all pending publications. Unclaimed items will not appear in your FAR. My CV continuously imports records from external databases. These appear in your Publications Queue and require your attention.

Missing a publication? Add it manually, or use AI-assisted data entry to paste text from your CV, manuscript, or grant letter and let My CV extract the structured data automatically. This feature also works for grants, professional activities, and teaching activities.

2
Year-Round
Service & Professional Activities

Keep your service and professional activities current. Since these activities are entered manually, this is a great place to use AI-assisted data entry — paste text from your CV or other documents and let My CV extract and categorize the information automatically. The feature will also flag any potential duplicates before they are added.

Ongoing roles carry forward automatically. If a committee or service role has no end date, My CV includes it in every FAR until you add one. Before reporting season, review your existing entries and add end dates to any roles that have ended.

3
January – February
Ready to Submit

Your profile is pre-populated when you open your reports. Review it before you click Start.

  1. Review your pre-populated profile for completeness and accuracy.
  2. Click Start on Step 1: Faculty Annual Report (FAR) — required for your Annual Faculty Merit Review.
  3. Click Start on Step 2: External & International Activity Report (EIAR) — required for Conflict of Commitment Policy compliance.
  4. Submit before your unit deadline.
Good to know: External activities you have already entered for the FAR can be selected in the EIAR without re-entry. You do not need to enter them twice.
Two reports, two paths. The FAR and EIAR open at the same time but follow separate review routes. The FAR goes through your department and school for Annual Faculty Merit Review. The EIAR is reviewed for Conflict of Commitment Policy compliance. Completing one does not complete the other — both must be submitted before your unit deadline.

About My CV

My CV (Symplectic Elements, provided by Digital Science) is Boston University’s faculty research profiling system. Faculty use My CV to maintain a comprehensive record of their scholarly and creative works, grants, service activities, teaching, and professional activities.

In addition to generating the FAR and EIAR, My CV supports Open Access Policy compliance through its integration with OpenBU, BU’s institutional repository. Faculty can also connect their ORCID iD to My CV to enable automated publication syncing from external databases.

My CV is accessed at mycv.bu.edu.
